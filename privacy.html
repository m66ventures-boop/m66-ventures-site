Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 June 2026

M66 Ventures, LLC (“M66 Ventures,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through our website at m66ventures.com (the “Site”), how we use it, and the choices you have. M66 Ventures is a holding company; each portfolio company operates its own products and maintains its own privacy practices.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you provide — when you submit our contact form, we collect your name, email address, optional company name, and the message you send.
  • Technical data — our hosting and infrastructure providers automatically log basic technical information (such as IP address, browser type, and pages viewed) to operate and secure the Site.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiries and communicate with you.
  • To operate, maintain, and secure the Site.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. How your information is stored

Contact submissions are stored in a secure database (Supabase), and the Site is hosted by Vercel. We retain submissions only as long as needed to respond to and manage your inquiry.

4. Third-party services

We rely on a small number of reputable service providers to run the Site, including Vercel (hosting), Supabase (database), and Google Fonts (typography). Links to our portfolio companies’ websites are governed by their own privacy policies.

5. Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the information you submitted to us by emailing legal@m66ventures.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

6. Children

The Site is intended for a general business audience and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above reflects the most recent revision.

8. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email legal@m66ventures.com.
